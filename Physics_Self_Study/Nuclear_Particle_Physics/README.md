# Nuclear & Particle Physics Review

Combined experimental + theoretical review of nuclear physics (structure, decay, reactions) and particle physics (Standard Model, QCD, electroweak, heavy ions), covering Nuclear Physics I and II. Part D adds astrophysics and nuclear astrophysics for the astro prelim, assuming no prior astronomy course.

13-week self-paced plan (~3 months, 3–4 chapters/week), plus an optional 4-week astro/nuclear-astro Part D (Weeks 14–17), using textbooks from my OneDrive `3_Textbooks` library.
