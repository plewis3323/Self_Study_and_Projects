## Part D — Astrophysics & nuclear astrophysics (astro prelim, no prior astro class)

### Main textbook: get one
| Priority | Book | Why |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ 📕 | Carroll & Ostlie, *An Introduction to Modern Astrophysics*, 2nd ed. | The standard astro-prelim reference; covers everything in Weeks 14–17 |
| ⭐ 🆓 | OpenStax, *Astronomy* 2e (openstax.org, free) | Free, conceptual (non-calculus) vocabulary builder for Week 14: start here if I've never had astro |
| 📕 | Choudhuri, *Astrophysics for Physicists* (Cambridge) | Written for physics grad students; compact and rigorous |
| 📕 | Maoz, *Astrophysics in a Nutshell* (Princeton) | Short, problem-driven; good for prelim-style practice |
| 📕 | Ryden & Peterson, *Foundations of Astrophysics* | Clear undergraduate alternative to Carroll & Ostlie |

### Free courses and notes
- 🆓 MIT OpenCourseWare 8.282J *Introduction to Astronomy* (Week 14 basics).
- 🆓 MIT OpenCourseWare 8.901 *Astrophysics I* (stars and compact objects).
- 🆓 MIT OpenCourseWare 8.286 *The Early Universe* (Alan Guth; Week 17).
- 🆓 Onno Pols, *Stellar Structure and Evolution* lecture notes (Utrecht; free PDF). These cover Weeks 14–16.
- 🆓 Daniel Baumann, *Cosmology* lecture notes (Cambridge Part III; free on his website). These cover Week 17.
- 🆓 PDG reviews (pdg.lbl.gov): *Big-Bang Cosmology*, *Big-Bang Nucleosynthesis*, *Cosmic Rays*, *Dark Matter*, *Neutrinos in Cosmology*.

### By week
- **Week 14 — Astronomy & stellar physics from scratch:**
  - ⭐ Carroll & Ostlie Ch. 3 (continuous spectrum), Ch. 7 (binaries), Ch. 8 (classification of stellar spectra, Saha), Ch. 9–10 (stellar atmospheres and interiors).
  - 🆓 OpenStax *Astronomy* (stars and distance-ladder chapters).
  - 🆓 Pols notes (stellar structure equations).
- **Week 15 — Thermonuclear rates & stellar burning:**
  - ⭐ 📕 Iliadis, *Nuclear Physics of Stars* (Wiley): the modern standard for reaction rates.
  - 📕 Rolfs & Rodney, *Cauldrons in the Cosmos*: the experimental side.
  - 📕 Clayton, *Principles of Stellar Evolution and Nucleosynthesis* (classic).
  - 🆓 Adelberger et al., *Solar fusion cross sections II*, arXiv:1004.2318.
- **Week 16 — Heavy elements, stellar death & compact objects:**
  - ⭐ 📕 Burbidge, Burbidge, Fowler & Hoyle, *Synthesis of the Elements in Stars*, Rev. Mod. Phys. 29 (1957) 547 (B²FH, the founding paper).
  - 🆓 Cowan et al., *Origin of the heaviest elements: the rapid neutron-capture process*, arXiv:1901.01410.
  - 🆓 Janka, *Explosion mechanisms of core-collapse supernovae*, arXiv:1206.2503.
  - 🆓 Lattimer & Prakash, *The physics of neutron stars*, arXiv:astro-ph/0405262.
  - 📕 Shapiro & Teukolsky, *Black Holes, White Dwarfs, and Neutron Stars*: the reference for compact objects and TOV.
  - 📕 Kippenhahn, Weigert & Weiss, *Stellar Structure and Evolution*.
- **Week 17 — Cosmology, BBN & cosmic rays:**
  - ⭐ 📕 Ryden, *Introduction to Cosmology*, 2nd ed.: short and the usual prelim-level text.
  - 🆓 Baumann notes.
  - 🆓 Cyburt, Fields, Olive & Yeh, *Big Bang Nucleosynthesis: 2015*, arXiv:1505.01076.
  - 🆓 PDG *Cosmic Rays* and *Big-Bang Nucleosynthesis* reviews.
