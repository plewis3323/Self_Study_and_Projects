# Relativistic Heavy-Ion & Cold QCD

Self-paced graduate course on QCD matter. **Hot QCD:** the quark–gluon plasma, heavy-ion collisions and the sPHENIX program at RHIC. **Cold QCD:** nucleon structure, spin, partons in nuclei, gluon saturation and the Electron–Ion Collider (EIC/ePIC).

16 units over 3–5 months (fast / standard / extended tracks; see the syllabus). It uses textbooks from my OneDrive `3_Textbooks` library, mainly Bartke, the Particle Physics Reference Library Vols. 1–3, Thomson, Povh and Landau–Lifshitz Vol. 6. Open-access review articles cover topics the library lacks, such as the EIC White Paper and Yellow Report, and reviews of Glauber modeling, flow, jet quenching and the Color Glass Condensate.

Prerequisites: `../Nuclear_Particle_Physics/` (Weeks 8–13) and `../Statistical_Mechanics/`.

| Part | Units | Topic |
|---|---|---|
| A | 1–4 | Foundations: kinematics, QCD, partons and DIS, hadronization |
| B | 5–11 | Hot QCD matter: phase diagram, Glauber, bulk, hydro and flow, hard probes |
| C | 12–13 | Experiment: the sPHENIX detector and analysis methods |
| D | 14–16 | Cold QCD and the EIC: spin, TMDs and GPDs, saturation |
