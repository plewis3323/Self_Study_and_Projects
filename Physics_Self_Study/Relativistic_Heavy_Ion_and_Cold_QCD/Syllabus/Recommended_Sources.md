# Recommended Sources (not in my library)

These fill the gaps where my OneDrive `3_Textbooks` library is thin. They are organized by unit and complement the in-library readings and arXiv supplements in [Syllabus.md](Syllabus.md).

**Legend:**
- 🆓 = free / open access (arXiv numbers given; search them on arxiv.org or inspirehep.net).
- 📕 = book to buy or borrow (check the OU library / interlibrary loan first).
- ⭐ = top pick for that unit if I only get one thing.

## Books worth getting (whole course)

| Priority | Book | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| ⭐ 📕 | Yagi, Hatsuda & Miake, *Quark–Gluon Plasma* (Cambridge) | The closest thing to a standard heavy-ion textbook: thermodynamics, lattice, hydrodynamics, all major observables (Units 5–11) |
| ⭐ 📕 | Florkowski, *Phenomenology of Ultra-Relativistic Heavy-Ion Collisions* (World Scientific) | Glauber, hydrodynamics, freeze-out, HBT, with explicit derivations (Units 6–9) |

## By unit

### Part A — Foundations
- **Unit 1 (landscape):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Busza, Rajagopal & van der Schee, arXiv:1802.04801 (already in the syllabus).
  - 📕 Florkowski Ch. 1–2 (kinematics, nuclear geometry).
- **Unit 2 (QCD):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Skands, arXiv:1207.2389.
  - 🆓 Salam, arXiv:1011.5131.
  - 📕 Ellis–Stirling–Webber Ch. 1–2.
- **Unit 3 (partons and DIS):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Gao, Harland-Lang & Rojo, *The structure of the proton in the LHC precision era*, arXiv:1709.04922.
  - 🆓 EPPS21 nuclear PDFs (Eskola et al.), arXiv:2112.12462.
  - 📕 Devenish & Cooper-Sarkar.
- **Unit 4 (hadronization and generators):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Bierlich et al., *A comprehensive guide to the physics and usage of PYTHIA 8.3*, arXiv:2203.11601.
  - 📕 Andersson, Gustafson, Ingelman & Sjöstrand, *Parton fragmentation and string dynamics*, Phys. Rep. 97 (1983) 31 (the Lund model).
  - 📕 Wang & Gyulassy, *HIJING*, Phys. Rev. D 44 (1991) 3501.

### Part B — Hot QCD matter
- **Unit 5 (thermodynamics and phase diagram):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Laine & Vuorinen, arXiv:1701.01554.
  - 🆓 HotQCD (Bazavov et al.), *Equation of state in (2+1)-flavor QCD*, arXiv:1407.6387.
  - 📕 Yagi–Hatsuda–Miake Part I.
- **Unit 6 (Glauber and centrality):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Miller et al., arXiv:nucl-ex/0701025 (already in the syllabus).
  - 🆓 Loizides, Kamin & d'Enterria, *Improved Monte Carlo Glauber predictions* (TGlauberMC), arXiv:1710.07098, which comes with ROOT code I can run.
- **Unit 7 (bulk production and freeze-out):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Andronic, Braun-Munzinger, Redlich & Stachel, *Decoding the phase structure of QCD via particle production at high energy*, arXiv:1710.09425.
  - 📕 Letessier & Rafelski.
- **Unit 8 (hydrodynamics and flow):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Ollitrault, *Relativistic hydrodynamics for heavy-ion collisions*, arXiv:0708.2433 (pedagogical, short).
  - 🆓 Jeon & Heinz, *Introduction to Hydrodynamics*, arXiv:1503.03931.
  - 🆓 Romatschke & Romatschke, *Relativistic Fluid Dynamics In and Out of Equilibrium*, arXiv:1712.05815 (full book).
  - 📕 Florkowski.
- **Unit 9 (femtoscopy and fluctuations):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Lisa, Pratt, Soltz & Wiedemann, *Femtoscopy in relativistic heavy ion collisions*, arXiv:nucl-ex/0505014.
  - 🆓 Luo & Xu, *Search for the QCD critical point with fluctuations of conserved quantities*, arXiv:1701.02105.
- **Unit 10 (jets and jet quenching):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Cao & Wang, *Jet quenching and medium response in high-energy heavy-ion collisions: a review*, arXiv:2002.04028.
  - 🆓 Salam, *Towards Jetography*, arXiv:0906.1833.
  - 🆓 Cacciari, Salam & Soyez, *The anti-k_t jet clustering algorithm*, arXiv:0802.1189.
  - 🆓 FastJet user manual, arXiv:1111.6097.
- **Unit 11 (heavy flavor, quarkonia and EM probes):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Rothkopf, *Heavy quarkonium in extreme conditions*, arXiv:1912.02253.
  - 📕 Matsui & Satz, Phys. Lett. B 178 (1986) 416 (the original J/ψ-suppression paper).
  - 📕 Vogt.
  - 📕 Kapusta & Gale (thermal photon and dilepton rates).

### Part C — Experiment
- **Unit 12 (sPHENIX detector):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 sPHENIX Technical Design Report and Beam Use Proposals (public on the sPHENIX/BNL site).
  - 🆓 Fabjan & Gianotti, *Calorimetry for particle physics*, Rev. Mod. Phys. 75 (2003) 1243.
  - 🆓 Frühwirth & Strandlie.
  - 📕 Wigmans.
- **Unit 13 (analysis methods):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Bohm & Zech.
  - 🆓 PDG *Statistics* review.
  - 📕 Bernhard, Moreland & Bass, *Bayesian estimation of the specific shear and bulk viscosity of quark–gluon plasma*, Nature Phys. 15 (2019) 1113.

### Part D — Cold QCD and the EIC
- **Unit 14 (spin):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Aidala, Bass, Hasch & Mallot, *The spin structure of the nucleon*, arXiv:1209.2803.
  - 📕 Leader.
- **Unit 15 (TMDs and GPDs):**
  - ⭐ 🆓 Boussarie et al., *TMD Handbook*, arXiv:2304.03302 (a full free book).
  - 🆓 Belitsky & Radyushkin, *Unraveling hadron structure with generalized parton distributions*, arXiv:hep-ph/0504030.
- **Unit 16 (saturation and the EIC):**
  - ⭐ 📕 Kovchegov & Levin.
  - 🆓 Iancu & Venugopalan, *The color glass condensate and high-energy scattering in QCD*, arXiv:hep-ph/0303204 (pedagogical).
  - 🆓 EIC Conceptual Design Report (BNL/JLab, public).
  - 🆓 ePIC collaboration public detector documentation.
