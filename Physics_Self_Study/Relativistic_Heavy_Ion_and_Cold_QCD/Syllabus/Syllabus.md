# Relativistic Heavy-Ion & Cold QCD — Self-Paced Course Syllabus

**Scope:** a graduate-level course on QCD matter. It covers **hot QCD**: the quark–gluon plasma, heavy-ion collisions at RHIC and the LHC, and the **sPHENIX** program. It also covers **cold QCD**: nucleon structure, spin, partons in nuclei, gluon saturation, and the **Electron–Ion Collider (EIC/ePIC)**. Theory, phenomenology and experimental technique are covered together.
**Length:** 16 units, self-paced over 3–5 months (see *Pacing* below).
**Prerequisites:** `../../Nuclear_Particle_Physics/` Weeks 8–13 (quark model, QED, DIS, QCD), and `../../Statistical_Mechanics/` (quantum gases, phase transitions). Units 2–3 here are a fast refresher if those are done.
**Weekly rhythm (one unit ≈ one week):**
1. Read the core chapters.
2. Write notes in `../Notes/Unit_XX.md`.
3. Work the AI-generated practice set in `../Exercises/Unit_XX/`.
4. Use the cross-references when a topic needs a second explanation.
5. Read the open-access supplements. Several topics (especially cold QCD and the EIC) are thin in the textbook library, so for those units the review articles are core reading.
6. **Deep-dive track (optional):** field theory, kinetic theory and hydrodynamics at a deeper level. It doesn't count toward the unit's core.

### Pacing

| Track | Length | How |
|---|---|---|
| Fast | ~3 months (13 weeks) | Skim Units 2–3 if the Nuclear & Particle review is done. Skip the ★ units (9 and 13). |
| Standard | ~4 months (16 weeks) | One unit per week. |
| Extended | ~5 months (20 weeks) | Take two weeks each for Units 8, 10, 15 and 16, and do the deep-dive track. |

## Part A — Foundations (Units 1–4)

### Unit 1 — The landscape: kinematics, nuclei, and colliders
- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 1 (Introduction), Ch. 3 (Basic Properties of Atomic Nuclei), Ch. 4 (Sources of Relativistic Nuclei)
  - PRL-Th §7.1 (Introduction to nucleus–nucleus collisions)
  - PRL-Acc §10.2 (RHIC), §6.9 (Ion Colliders)
- **Cross-ref:**
  - PRL-Acc §6.4 (Luminosity)
  - Povh Ch. 5 (Geometric Shapes of Nuclei)
- **Supplements:** BRvdS (introduction and overview).
- **Goals:**
  - Rapidity, pseudorapidity, transverse mass, √s_NN, Feynman x.
  - DIS variables: Bjorken x, Q², y, W.
  - Nuclear density profiles (Woods–Saxon) and Fermi momentum.
  - RHIC, LHC and EIC machine parameters; species and energies; why collide nuclei.

### Unit 2 — QCD essentials (refresher)
- **Core:**
  - Thomson Ch. 10 (QCD)
  - PRL-Th Ch. 4.1–4.4 (massless QCD, renormalization group, asymptotic freedom, running coupling)
  - Griffiths Ch. 8 (selected: color, QCD Feynman rules)
- **Cross-ref:**
  - Gauge notes (local SU(3) → gluon self-coupling)
  - H&M Ch. 2 (symmetries and quarks)
  - PRL-Th Ch. 2.5
- **Deep-dive (optional):**
  - Schwartz Ch. 25–26 (Yang–Mills)
  - P&S Ch. 16 (non-Abelian theory)
- **Goals:**
  - SU(3) color and color factors (C_F, C_A, T_F).
  - β-function, Λ_QCD, running α_s.
  - Confinement vs. asymptotic freedom.
  - Chiral symmetry and its breaking; where hadron mass comes from.

### Unit 3 — Partons, DIS and nuclei at high energy (bridge to cold QCD)
- **Core:**
  - Thomson Ch. 7 (Elastic Scattering), Ch. 8 (Deep Inelastic Scattering)
  - Povh Ch. 7 (DIS), Ch. 8 (Quarks, Gluons, and the Strong Interaction), especially §8.3 (scaling violations) and §8.5 (nuclear effects in DIS)
  - Companion Parts I–II
- **Cross-ref:**
  - H&M Ch. 8–10
  - Griffiths §8.3–8.6
  - PRL-Th §4.5 (hard processes)
  - PDG *Structure Functions* review
- **Deep-dive (optional):**
  - P&S Ch. 14, 17
  - Schwartz Ch. 32
- **Goals:**
  - Form factors and the Rosenbluth formula.
  - F₁, F₂ and Callan–Gross; Bjorken scaling.
  - PDFs, DGLAP, and the rise of the gluon at small x.
  - Nuclear PDFs: shadowing, anti-shadowing, the EMC effect, Fermi motion.

### Unit 4 — Hadronization, fragmentation and event generators
- **Core:**
  - Hadronization readings part1–part7 (framing, jets, generators, FFs, Schwinger mechanism, Lund string, DPM/pomeron)
  - Companion Part III (fragmentation functions)
  - Thomson §10.6 (two-jet kinematics excerpt, `Thomson_10_6_Two_Jet_Kinematics.pdf`)
- **Cross-ref:**
  - PRL-Th §4.5
  - PDG *Fragmentation Functions* review
  - Maña Ch. 3 (Monte Carlo methods)
  - Bartke Ch. 7 (fragmentation processes)
- **Computing tie-in:** `Coding_SWE_Physics-SIM_AI_ML_Space/Pythia/`, `HIJING/`.
- **Goals:**
  - Factorization: σ = PDF ⊗ partonic σ̂ ⊗ FF.
  - z and the scaling of D(z, Q²).
  - String breaking and the Schwinger rate.
  - What PYTHIA and HIJING do: MPI, ISR/FSR, minijets, and the nuclear geometry in HIJING.

## Part B — Hot QCD Matter (Units 5–11)

### Unit 5 — QCD thermodynamics and the phase diagram
- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 2 (Quantum Chromodynamics and the Phase Transition)
  - PRL-Th Ch. 5.1–5.2, 5.4, 5.6 (lattice QCD, confinement and string breaking, chiral symmetry breaking)
  - Hadronization reading part8 (bag model and QGP)
  - Pathria §7.3 (blackbody radiation → Stefan–Boltzmann gas of gluons and quarks), Ch. 9 (thermodynamics of the early universe)
- **Cross-ref:**
  - Povh §20.3 (phases of nuclear matter), §20.4 (early universe)
  - Pathria Ch. 12–14 (criticality, universality: the QCD critical point and the Ising class)
  - BRvdS (phase diagram and lattice sections)
  - PDG *Quark–Gluon Plasma* review
- **Deep-dive (optional):**
  - Altland §4.2 (field integral for the partition function → imaginary time and Matsubara frequencies)
- **Goals:**
  - Bag-model estimate of T_c.
  - Counting degrees of freedom: ε/T⁴ for a hadron gas vs. a QGP.
  - Lattice results: crossover at μ_B = 0, T_pc ≈ 155 MeV.
  - Polyakov loop and chiral condensate as order parameters.
  - Hagedorn temperature; μ_B and the conjectured critical point.

### Unit 6 — Collision geometry, Glauber model and centrality
- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 6 (Cross Sections and Collision Geometry), Ch. 7 (Fragmentation Processes)
  - PRL-Th §7.2 (bulk hadron production, initial conditions)
- **Supplements:** Glauber (core reading for this unit).
- **Cross-ref:**
  - Povh Ch. 5
  - HNC Ch. 3 (heavy-ion reaction mechanisms)
- **Computing tie-in:** `Coding_SWE_Physics-SIM_AI_ML_Space/ROOT/` (a Glauber Monte Carlo is a natural ROOT project).
- **Goals:**
  - Impact parameter, N_part, N_coll and thickness functions T_A, T_AB.
  - Optical vs. Monte Carlo Glauber; eccentricity ε_n.
  - Centrality determination (sPHENIX MBD/ZDC).
  - Bjorken energy density ε_Bj; spectators and electromagnetic dissociation.

### Unit 7 — Bulk particle production and statistical hadronization
- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 8 (Multiplicities and Relative Abundances), Ch. 9 (Longitudinal Distributions), Ch. 10 (Transverse Spectra), Ch. 12.1 (Strangeness), Ch. 13 (Light Nuclei, Antinuclei, Hypernuclei)
  - PRL-Th §7.2–7.3 (bulk production; hadronization and hadronic freeze-out)
- **Supplements:** PHENIX-WP and STAR-WP (bulk-observable sections).
- **Cross-ref:** Bartke Ch. 11 (electromagnetic effects on meson spectra).
- **Goals:**
  - dN_ch/dη and its scaling with N_part.
  - m_T scaling; the blast-wave model (T_kin, ⟨β_T⟩).
  - Chemical vs. kinetic freeze-out; the statistical hadronization model (T_ch, μ_B).
  - Strangeness enhancement; baryon/meson anomaly and coalescence.

### Unit 8 — Relativistic hydrodynamics and collective flow
- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 15 (Collective Flow)
  - PRL-Th §7.4 (elliptic flow)
  - L&L 6 Ch. XV §§133–136 (relativistic fluid dynamics; viscosity and heat conduction)
- **Supplements:** H&S (core reading for this unit); BRvdS (hydrodynamics and liquid-QGP sections).
- **Cross-ref:**
  - Statistical_Mechanics review (transport and fluctuations units)
  - Pathria Ch. 15 (fluctuations and nonequilibrium)
- **Deep-dive (optional):**
  - L&L 10 Ch. I (Boltzmann equation, transport coefficients)
  - Altland §10.3 (Boltzmann kinetic theory)
- **Goals:**
  - T^{μν} for an ideal and a viscous fluid.
  - Bjorken (boost-invariant) flow solution.
  - η/s and the KSS bound (1/4π); bulk viscosity.
  - Fourier harmonics v_n; event-plane vs. cumulant methods; non-flow.
  - Number-of-constituent-quark scaling.
  - Small systems: p/d/³He+Au geometry scan.

### Unit 9 ★ — Femtoscopy, fluctuations and correlations (optional on the fast track)
- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 14 (Hadronic Femtoscopy)
  - PRL-Th §7.7 (fluctuation and correlation signals)
- **Cross-ref:**
  - Pathria Ch. 12, 15 (critical fluctuations)
  - Bartke Ch. 21 (more about QGP)
  - BRvdS (critical-point sections)
- **Goals:**
  - HBT radii (R_out, R_side, R_long) and the space–time picture.

### Unit 10 — Hard probes I: high-p_T hadrons, jets and jet quenching
- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 19 (High Transverse Momenta), Ch. 20 (Production and Absorption of Jets)
  - PRL-Th §7.5 (in-medium attenuation of high-p_T hadrons and jets)
  - Hadronization reading part2 (jets)
- **Supplements:** M&vL (introductory and energy-loss sections); sPHENIX proposal (jet and photon physics case); PHENIX-WP (high-p_T suppression section).
- **Cross-ref:**
  - PRL-Det §6.5 (jets and missing energy)
  - Thomson §10.6
- **Deep-dive (optional):** Schwartz Ch. 36 (jets and effective field theory).
- **Goals:**
  - R_AA, R_CP, I_AA; π⁰ R_AA (the PHENIX discovery and the sPHENIX program).
  - Radiative vs. collisional energy loss; BDMPS-Z at a qualitative level; q̂.
  - Jet algorithms (anti-k_T), underlying-event subtraction, jet R_AA, substructure.
  - γ–jet and di-jet asymmetry.

- **Core:**
  - Bartke Ch. 12.2 (Heavy Flavours), Ch. 16 (Charmonium Suppression), Ch. 17 (Di-Lepton Mass Spectrum), Ch. 18 (Direct Photons)
  - PRL-Th §7.6 (vector mesons and direct photons: penetrating probes)

- **Cross-ref:**
  - Thomson Ch. 10
  - Mann Ch. 18 (Heavy Quarks and QCD)
  - Bartke Ch. 22 (LHC predictions)
- **Supplements:** sPHENIX proposal (Upsilon and open-heavy-flavor physics case).

  - Thermal direct photons (effective temperature from the slope); the direct-photon puzzle.
  - Dileptons and ρ broadening; chiral restoration.
